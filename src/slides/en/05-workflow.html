IDENFLUNOVAProducts in operation

Follow the route from receiving to dispatch.

Follow the numbered stops to see which instructions and results move with the load.

  1. Receiving and induction

    Turn the inbound request into a task and check entry conditions.

  2. Storage and retrieval

    Store the load or retrieve the requested stock from the AS/RS.

  3. Transfer

    Move the load to the next process by conveyor, AGV or AMR.

  4. Sorting and dispatch

    Route the load to its destination in priority order.

Reporting backCompleted quantities and exceptions go back to the upstream system to determine the next task.
